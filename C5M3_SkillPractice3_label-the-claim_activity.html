Label the claim

Attribution is the work of determining how much of an observed change an initiative caused. Claim fidelity is the label that tells a reader how firmly the evidence supports that answer. A reviewer who catches one overstated label will doubt the next number too. Your job is to give each result the label its comparison can support.

A manufacturer’s service and warranty operation has run an AI drafting assistant for two quarters. In the same two quarters, a seasonal peak in claims came and went, and a change to how claims are routed went live a month after the assistant. Six results are headed for the quarterly review.

Label each result by reading the measure and the comparison behind it.

Attributed means a quantified change that can be assigned to the assistant with high confidence. Directional means the evidence shows which way the effect points and leaves its size open, because other causes share the period. Assumption means an effect nobody measured here, which the organization would be choosing to accept.
