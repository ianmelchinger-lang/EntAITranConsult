Eight initiatives across three horizons, approved in outline. You test it for feasibility, sequencing, and value, then rebuild it. A roadmap improved by cutting or deferring an initiative is a stronger roadmap.

Pressure-test first

Every claimed benefit has two addresses

Where the benefit lands

The function that books the saving, the faster cycle, the shorter queue.

Where the residual work lands

Whoever now verifies, corrects, escalates, or waits, wherever they sit.

A business case that reads only where the benefit lands is arithmetic, not analysis. Your model names both.

What you will produce here

Each piece becomes part of the advisory package you hand in at the end.

Transformation roadmap

Phased, with milestones and dependencies, sequenced by value and readiness rather than by enthusiasm or vendor pressure.

ROI business case

Costs, benefits, timing, and the assumptions finance will challenge, built so its honest conclusion could be “not on these numbers.”

Governance and risk plan

Risks named and mitigated, with each human-review control tested against the volume the reviewer will actually face.

How the work is judged

One rubric, four dimensions, applied to the finished package. This module's work shows up most in the highlighted dimension.

What the rubric explicitly credits

  • A well-evidenced recommendation against, de-scope, or defer, scored as a correct and high-scoring outcome.
  • Naming where value lands and where the residual cost lands.
  • Testing an oversight control rather than asserting it.
  • Surfacing an unwelcome finding early and constructively.
