Build the whole set

A rank only means something against the population around it. Before anything is ranked, the set has to hold every candidate the organization actually has, stated in one unit: a business process and the benefit expected from changing it.

A manufacturer's transformation lead is building the candidate set for next year's AI budget. Material has come in from four places: the solicitation list, the intake log that records running AI work, a service team's shared channel, and the vendor file.

Decide where each piece of material belongs.

Enters as written means it already names a process and a benefit. Enters once restated means it's a real initiative described as a tool, a model, or a history. Stays out means it's evidence about a benefit rather than something to fund.
