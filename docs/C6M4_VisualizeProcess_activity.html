Assemble the account

Ten pieces of one integration, shown out of order. Put them back the way the work actually has to run.

A manufacturer’s steering committee has five advisory documents in front of it: an opportunity assessment, a readiness report, a roadmap, an ROI case, and a governance and risk plan. Each was signed off on its own. The committee could not vote, and it has asked for the documents back as one account that ends in one recommendation.

Below are ten pieces of the path from five separate documents to that one account, out of order. Use the arrows to put them in the sequence the work requires. The order is the order of the work, which is not always the order in which the finished document is read. Where more than one order works, any of them will pass.
