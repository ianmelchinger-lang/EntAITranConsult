Hold the yardstick still

A fixed criteria set is the list of criteria and weights chosen before scoring begins and applied, unchanged, to every candidate. Once candidates are known, every sponsor has a reason the yardstick should move in their favor. Your job is to tell a fair correction from a moved yardstick.

A manufacturer's transformation team fixed its benefit criteria before reading any candidate: size of the benefit, weighted at 60 percent, and confidence in the evidence, weighted at 40 percent. Confidence reflects how well the organization's own records could check the claim. Effort is scored separately and isn't part of these requests.
