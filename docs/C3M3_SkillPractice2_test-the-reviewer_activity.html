Test the reviewer

Oversight is a design property, not a job title. It exists when one specific person has the information to recognize a problem, the authority to intervene, and the time to do it, before the output is filed, shipped, or relied on downstream. Underneath all three sits competence: the reviewer has to be able to judge whether the output is fit for use. Your job is to find what each reviewer lacks.

A manufacturer’s proposal says a person reviews each supplier-nonconformance write-up, the record of parts a supplier delivered outside specification, before it reaches the quality file, the controlled record. Below are five ways to staff that one review step. A careful review of one write-up takes about ten minutes.

For each reviewer, tick every condition the description shows they lack. If they lack none, choose Nothing missing.

Information: sees what is needed to recognize a problem, before the write-up moves on.

Authority: can stop or send back a write-up on their own say.

Time: has protected hours for the volume they face.

Competence: can judge whether a write-up is fit for use.

Second pass: move the volume
