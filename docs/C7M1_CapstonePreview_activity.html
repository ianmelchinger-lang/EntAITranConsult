Your client

A multinational manufacturer with a full set of corporate functions behind its plants. It is not a technology company and it is not starting fresh: it carries legacy systems, plants used to running their own way, and AI work that began from the bottom up. Its leaders want a prioritized plan. You will meet its people by role, not by name.

Analysis in skeleton form

Some upstream analysis arrives in skeleton form, so you apply and connect methods rather than start from a blank page. The room also carries the ordinary complications of a real enterprise, so a strong package cannot be built by approving everything.

The judgment at the heart of this module

Eighteen candidates are not eighteen good ideas. Your work is to narrow them with a method you can explain, and then ask whether the organization is ready for what survives.

What you will produce here

Each piece becomes part of the advisory package you hand in at the end.

Opportunity assessment

Every candidate stated the same way and held to one measure, with the evidence behind each judgment visible.

Prioritized opportunity portfolio

A ranked, balanced mix across value, risk, and effort, with a reason a steering group can repeat for every place on it.

Readiness summary

An honest reading of data, technology, and people. “Not yet, and here is what would change that” is a legitimate finding.
