Match the instrument

Three instruments get called “the AI inventory” inside the same week, and they don't answer the same question.

A capability inventory tracks what one specific capability lets the organization do, and how well it does it. A maturity assessment folds many signals into one score for how ready the organization is, as a whole, to run AI at all. A regulatory register lists only the systems that trigger an external duty, a risk classification, or a reporting requirement — nothing else.
