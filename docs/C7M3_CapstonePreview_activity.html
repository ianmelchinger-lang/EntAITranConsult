The advisory package

One coherent document. The roadmap funds what the portfolio chose; the business case prices what the roadmap schedules; the governance plan covers what the roadmap deploys.

The executive presentation

The same argument, shaped for senior leaders who will decide in the room.

The hardest finding, delivered to the person who least wants it

Early

While there is still time to act on it, not after the plan is committed.

Without hedging

Stated plainly enough that it cannot be read as a caveat and set aside.

Without theatre

Framed as a decision the executive can make, not a crisis to react to.
