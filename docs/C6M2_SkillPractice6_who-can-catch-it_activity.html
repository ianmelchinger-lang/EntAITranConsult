Who can catch it

Responsibility is misattributed when the person nearest an automated system takes the blame for a failure that the system’s design produced. A control that names a person is a control only if that person has a realistic means of catching the error. There are three. When one is missing, the control is a design defect, and the fix is to redesign it so that the person has what is missing, not to blame the person who was named.

  1. Time: enough set aside to do the reading.
  2. Source: the source material beside the draft.
  3. Authority: the power to halt the process.

A manufacturer’s AI plan names a person beside each of five controls. Each line below states what the design gives that person. Read it against the three means and use only what the line says.

Each control lacks at most one means. Choose “Holds” when the line gives the person all three. Otherwise choose the means it lacks, then choose the redesign that supplies it.
