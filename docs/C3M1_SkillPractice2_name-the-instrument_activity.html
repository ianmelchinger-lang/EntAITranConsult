Name the instrument

A passing test is persuasive, and it covers one thing. Testing shows whether a model does its narrow task correctly. Only a structural inventory or a governance review can show whether the organization around the model can support it safely. Those instruments ask people who owns, checks, trains, and touches what. Ask which instrument produced a piece of evidence, and you know which question it can answer.

A sponsor says the warranty-claims assistant is ready for all four regions and offers six pieces of evidence. Every piece is accurate as described. The question is what each one tells you.

Choose what each piece tells you.

About the model means it shows whether the assistant does its narrow task correctly. About the organization means it shows whether the people, data, and processes around the assistant can support its use. About neither means it reports on something else, such as whether the platform is running or how much work is moving.
