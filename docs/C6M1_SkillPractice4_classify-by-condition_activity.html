Classify by condition, not by feel

Under a risk-tiered law, a category such as high-risk is a legal classification that anyone can check. Enumerable conditions trigger it. It does not describe how dangerous a system feels. Two reviewers who rate by feel can disagree, and neither can show why. Two reviewers who check the same list of conditions reach the same category and name the same trigger.

A manufacturer’s transformation team has five systems to classify. Each card gives the system’s intended use in one sentence.

First, by feel. Rate how dangerous each system feels, then lock your ratings.
