Who would see the change?

Governance does not end at approval. After launch, data shifts, users change how they work with the tool, and operating conditions move. A launch approval reviewed the system as it stood on launch day, so only watching during operation can show what has changed since. A signal is a number or observation that someone checks on a schedule. A watcher is the named role who checks it and would act on a change.

A manufacturer’s demand-forecasting assistant went live at its Ohio plant and has now run for five months. The approval record covers the system as it stood on launch day. Seven things have changed since.
