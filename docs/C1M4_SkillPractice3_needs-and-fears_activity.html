Whose need, whose fear?

Identifying who is touched by a system is the easy half of a stakeholder map. The second half is discovering what each of them needs and fears — and a role title never answers that on its own.

Three roles at a multinational manufacturer are named below, all touched by the same AI engagement. Below them are six statements, shuffled. Place each one under the role it actually belongs to.

Sponsoring executive — accountable for the funding decision and the figure already reported to the board.
Quality engineer — verifies inspection output and answers for any escape that reaches a customer.
Maintenance planner — owns the work-order schedule the automation feeds.
