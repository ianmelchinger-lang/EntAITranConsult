Run the two-reader check

Case credibility is the trust a reader places in the claims a case makes. Two readers test it in different ways. A finance reader looks for numbers stated against a baseline, which is the current state expressed as a number, and takes the first scenario they meet as the claim. A sponsor looks for the strategic argument and wants to follow it without doing arithmetic. A case earns both readers by keeping the rationale apart from the numbers, and by putting the conservative scenario ahead of the upside.

An advisor's summary for an AI assistant that helps inspectors at two of a manufacturer's plants catch defects has six blocks. An escaped defect is a faulty unit that passes inspection and leaves the plant.

Three blocks carry the strategic rationale. Three carry numbers: the baseline, a conservative scenario, and an upside. In the draft below, they are interleaved.
