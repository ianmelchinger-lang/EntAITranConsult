Whose ledger

Every benefit has a size and a destination. The destination is its landing line: the financial line where the benefit appears, together with the owner accountable for that line. A case that states a size without a destination has answered half of the reviewer’s question. Producing a benefit and receiving it are also separate gates. Hours returned to a team remain capacity until a headcount plan or a contract changes.

A manufacturer’s quality organization lead would fund a system that reads supplier quality documents from the quality budget. The case lists five benefits. Every figure on this page is illustrative.

Put two questions to each benefit. What has to change before it arrives, and has anyone agreed to change it? Whose ledger then receives it?
