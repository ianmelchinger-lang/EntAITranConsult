The one-sentence test

Answer-first structure puts the decision requested at the opening of a communication, before any of the argument. A reader under time pressure decides within the first few seconds whether to give a document attention, and the writer who must produce that opening has to have resolved the recommendation first. An opening that cannot be written is a sign the position has not been settled.

A manufacturer’s executive committee will be asked whether to extend an automated maintenance-scheduling system from two plants to seven. You are the advisor, and the analysis is finished. The facts below are all you have, and they do not all lean one way.

First, decide what you recommend. Then build the sentence you would say first to the committee. The decision goes in that sentence. The argument does not.
