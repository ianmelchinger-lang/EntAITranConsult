Reopen the answer that missed the question

Reviewer independence means testing a plan you had no part in writing. The reason is structural and says nothing about anyone’s honesty: finishing a plan requires believing it works, so the authors are thorough about the risks they weighed and blind to the ones they did not. An outsider can reopen a question the authors closed, but only some of their answers deserve it.

A reviewer who did not write a manufacturer’s eighteen-month roadmap asked, for six of its dates, what would have to be true for the date to hold. The authors answered each one.

Read what has to be true, then the authors’ answer. Mark the row Reopen if the answer speaks to a different question than the one the date depends on. Mark it Leave settled if the answer establishes what the date needs.

Second pass: improve the answer

For each row you would reopen, compare the authors’ answer with a revised one that speaks to what the date needs. Choose an answer and watch the mark change. Nothing here is checked.
