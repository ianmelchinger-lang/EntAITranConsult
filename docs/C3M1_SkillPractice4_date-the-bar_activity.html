Date the bar

A readiness finding compares an organization with a standard on a date. The standard tells a later reader what the finding was measured against, and the date tells them how far things have moved since. Some measures never need this, because minutes per claim stay minutes per claim. A readiness rating does need it, because the standard moves as the technology moves. Here, the bar means the standard a rating was measured against.

A manufacturer’s steering group has inherited six findings from earlier assessments. Each is shown as it was recorded. The findings are illustrative.

Decide what each finding lets you say today.

Means what it meant is a measure that stays the same over time, so you can read it without a bar or a date. Readable against its bar and date means you can interpret it, but only by holding it to the bar and date it records. Cannot be interpreted means the page is missing its bar, its date, or a bar specific enough to rebuild.
