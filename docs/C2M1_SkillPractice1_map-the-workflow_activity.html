Ready to compare, or not yet a candidate?

A workflow that can be walked on paper, step by step, with someone named at every point, is ready to be measured against anything else. A workflow that can't be walked this way isn't a weak candidate. It's a different job — mapping — and that job has to finish first.

Each of these six workflows arrived as a candidate AI opportunity. Decide whether it can actually be walked, step by step, with a named owner at every point, or whether a step in it still has nobody attached.
