Trace the control

Integration means reconciling separately written documents into one account that ends in one recommendation. You can check one document against another only once you know the question each exists to answer. What you are looking for is a cross-document contradiction: something one document requires to be true that another denies or leaves out, even though each document holds up on its own. A human control written into a governance plan is the sharpest case. Its minutes are real, so the business case has to cost them and the roadmap has to staff them.

A manufacturer is putting a drafting assistant into its supplier-nonconformance process at three European plants. Three documents in the advisory package bear on it. Each is shown with the question it exists to answer.

For each control in the governance plan, mark whether the ROI case costs its time and whether the roadmap staffs it. Then judge the control. A control is resolved only when all three documents carry it. If it is missing from either, it is an unresolved contradiction.
