Trustworthiness belongs to a system in context: a model together with the data feeding it, the people acting on its output, and the oversight arranged around it. Change any of the last three and you have a new arrangement, and the reasons the old one was trustworthy have to be established again. A few properties travel with the model itself. Knowing which is which tells you where evidence has to exist before use.

Suppose an earlier finding said a manufacturer’s warranty-claims assistant was trustworthy in its first region: the records were sound, the desk was trained, and a supervisor checked each determination before release. The transformation lead is now weighing eight changes, each on its own.

For each change, choose what it alters.

The data going in is what the assistant reads. The people acting on the output are the staff who use its determinations. The oversight around it is the check between output and action, and the route for what the assistant cannot handle.
