Find the residue

Every initiative has a ledger: where its claimed benefit is recorded, and where the verification, rework, or new expectation it leaves behind actually lands. When those two lines name the same desk, nothing has been transferred. When they name different desks, a gain in one place has been booked against a cost paid somewhere else.
