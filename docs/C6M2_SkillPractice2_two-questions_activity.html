Two questions about one system

Classifying a system means deciding which obligations attach to it, and two questions decide it. The regulatory question asks what the organization owes. Under the EU AI Act, a high-risk designation turns on stated conditions tied to the system’s intended purpose and to what its failure would do to health, safety, or fundamental rights. The authority question asks where in the deployment the exposure sits: what the system may do, and what stands between its output and an outcome. Counsel answers the first. Your plan answers the second. A fact that feels important may answer neither.
