Place each initiative by its evidence

An investment horizon is a funding lane, near, mid, or longer, and each lane has its own rules for how money is spent and reviewed. Two facts about an initiative decide its lane: how mature its proof of value is, and how directly it sits on the business the enterprise already runs and earns from. A start date decides neither.

A manufacturer’s transformation lead has six initiatives to place. The draft delivery plan gives each a start quarter, and you will see it on every card. Place the cards on the two facts.

Choose a lane for each card. The rule for this exercise:

Near needs both facts at full strength: a measured result from live use, on work the business runs and earns from today.

Longer needs neither: no proof yet, and today’s revenue does not lean on it.

Mid is everything between.

Second pass: change the sorting rule

Switch the rule from evidence to the calendar and watch the lanes move. Nothing here is checked.
