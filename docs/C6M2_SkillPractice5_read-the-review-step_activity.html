Read the review step

Reviewer complacency is the decline in a person’s vigilance as they check a system’s output. Practitioners differ about what sustains vigilance in review work. One account locates it in the consequence attached to the review: when a flag changes nothing, the reason to catch errors fades. Another locates it in workload and in how reliable the automation is: a system that is usually right trains attention away from itself, and a heavy queue speeds the drift. The two accounts point to different fixes, and a plan that addresses only one leaves the other operative.
