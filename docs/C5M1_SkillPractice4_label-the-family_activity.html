Label the family

Every benefit claim belongs to one of two families. An efficiency claim says the organization will do existing work cheaper or faster. A creation claim says it will produce capacity, offerings, or revenue that did not exist before. The families carry different evidence obligations, and a case that mixes them without labels invites the reviewer to apply the uncertainty of one family to the numbers of the other.

A manufacturer’s quality organization lists six benefits for a system that reads supplier quality documents. Every figure on this page is illustrative.

For each benefit, find what is measured today. If a recorded before exists to compare against, mark it efficiency. If the capacity, output, or revenue does not exist yet, mark it creation.

The case, grouped by family

Grouped this way, each family keeps the confidence its own evidence earns. Each claim lists the evidence that would confirm it and the month after go-live when that evidence will exist.

Second pass: choose when to measure

A case that measures too early counts only the benefits that have arrived. Choose the month of the first measurement. The totals count the claims whose evidence exists by then, at the values the case claims. Nothing here is checked.
