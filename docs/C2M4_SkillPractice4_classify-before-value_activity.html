Classify before you value

Regulatory exposure is the set of legal and oversight obligations a system carries once it runs. Where a regime sorts AI by risk, the higher tiers bring documentation, human oversight, and monitoring, and those duties cost money for as long as the system runs. Classify exposure first and that cost can change the order. Classify it last and a review can only approve or block what already sits at the top.

A manufacturer's transformation team has six candidates. Each card gives three facts: what the system decides, whom those decisions affect, and how much human review stands before the outcome.

Flag any candidate that makes decisions about people or acts without human review. Leave the rest unflagged.

The steering group's rule: consider candidates with an effort score of 3 or less, rank them by benefit, and take the top three. In this exercise, a flag adds 2 points of effort for documentation, review, and monitoring.
