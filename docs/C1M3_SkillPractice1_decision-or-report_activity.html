Artifact or report?

A document earns the name "artifact" only when a decision is riding on it — a specific person who will act differently depending on what it says. Everything else, however well it's written, is a report.

Each item below describes a document from an early-stage AI engagement. For each one, decide whether a real decision is riding on it, or whether it's a report — competent, maybe even necessary, but not built to make anyone say yes, no, or revise.
