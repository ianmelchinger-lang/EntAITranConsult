Answer first

Assembly order is the order in which you build an account: from the evidence upward, toward a conclusion you did not know when you began. Delivery order is the order in which you present it: the answer first, and everything after it in support of a decision the reader has already heard. The evidence is the same in both. What changes is the job each paragraph does, and a paragraph that does not support the answer belongs in an appendix.

A manufacturer’s after-sales organization tested a vendor’s service-triage assistant. The report below was written in the order the work was done, so the recommendation sits on its last line. You are preparing it for the steering committee.

Opening line: the answer the committee needs first. Mark exactly one paragraph.

Support: a reason for that answer. The committee would think less of the answer without it.

Appendix: something the work needed that the decision does not.
