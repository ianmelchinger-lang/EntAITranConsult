Mark the strength

A verified finding is something the assessor checked and can show. An asserted claim rests on what a stakeholder said or a document declared. Confidence, seniority, and how believable a source sounds decide neither label.

Five claims from the same inspection-tool assessment, each with the source behind it. Mark each one at the strength the source actually supports.

For each claim, decide whether the assessor can show a check behind it, or whether the assessor is relying on someone's word \u2014 spoken or written.
