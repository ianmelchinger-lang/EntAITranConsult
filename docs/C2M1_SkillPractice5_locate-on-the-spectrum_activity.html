One thing moves

Suppose the scheduling system no longer reassigns work orders itself — it only flags the ones it thinks are at risk, and a supervisor decides what to do. The system didn't get less capable. It just stopped being the one who acts — and that alone moves it from the heaviest governance load on the spectrum to the lightest of the two remaining categories: a probability someone still has to weigh.
