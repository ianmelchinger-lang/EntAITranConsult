Redeploy or revalidate?

A tool that learned patterns from one kind of example carries its correctness only as far as those examples reach — not one step further.

A quality documentation assistant has been drafting supplier-nonconformance write-ups at three plants for ninety days. Every draft is reviewed before filing, and the review has caught the cases where it hedges. A director now wants to point the same assistant at warranty-claims drafting instead — a different system, with four regions' worth of claims data that don't share the nonconformance data's format.

What's the right move?
